Frequently Asked Questions (Deer-Resistant in Coastal BC)

Why do deer avoid certain BC native plants?

Deer strongly dislike fuzzy, prickly, resinous, bitter, or strongly scented leaves — such as Nodding Onion, Western Yarrow, Pearly Everlasting, Sword Fern, Oregon-Grape, and Mock-Orange.

Will deer still browse deer-resistant young nursery transplants?

Yes — tender, heavily fertilized new shoots are often taste-tested in spring. Protect newly planted natives with lightweight chicken-wire cages for their first growing season until woody stems mature.

What deer-resistant native shrubs provide flowers for bees and hummingbirds?

Red-Flowering Currant, Mock-Orange, Nootka Rose, Snowberry, and Oregon-Grape deliver abundant spring nectar while generally shrugging off deer browsing.

Can I build a full multi-season deer-resistant border with only BC natives?

Absolutely: pair evergreen Sword Fern and Dull Oregon-Grape as structure, add spring-blooming Red-Flowering Currant and Camas, and finish with summer Western Yarrow, Pearly Everlasting, and Douglas Aster.
