3. Payment & Local Pickup

How In-Stock Orders & Seasonal Pre-Orders Work

  1. Submit your order or pre-order above to get your unique Order ID (e.g., WCPP-1042).
  2. In-Stock Plants: Send your Interac e-Transfer to willismdr@gmail.com with your Order ID, full name, and plant names in the memo, and we will confirm your Surrey pickup window.
  3. Seasonal Pre-Orders: Pre-orders reserve upcoming nursery batches (spring tomato/pepper starts, fall seed garlic, or potted native stock) ahead of release—you can pay via e-Transfer upon confirmation or ahead of pickup.

Surrey Live Plant Pickup Tips

Pickup location: 11531 97 Avenue, Surrey, BC (Royal Heights neighbourhood).
Please bring a shallow box, cardboard flat, or trunk tray so 4-inch pots and 1-gallon containers stay upright on the ride home. Avoid leaving live plants in a warm parked car during sunny afternoons.
