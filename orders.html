How Interac e-Transfer Payment Works

  1. Submit your order above to get your unique Order ID (e.g., WCPP-1042).
  2. Send your Interac e-Transfer to willismdr@gmail.com with your Order ID, full name, and plant names in the memo.
  3. Once your transfer arrives, your order row switches to Paid — Ready for Pickup on the Google Sheet and we confirm pickup timing by email or text.
