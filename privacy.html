Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

This policy explains how the West Coast Plant Project and the Surrey Royal Heights Seed & Book Library ("we") collect and use information on www.plantproject.ca. We operate from Surrey, British Columbia, and handle personal information in line with British Columbia's Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA) and Canada's PIPEDA.

1. Website analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 and Google Tag Manager to understand how visitors use the site, for example which plants, filters, search words, calculators, and pages are popular, how far pages are scrolled, and how fast pages load. This uses cookies and similar technologies and collects device, browser, approximate location (city level), and interaction information. Text typed into personal fields (names, emails, phone numbers, notes) is automatically replaced with [REDACTED_PII] before any analytics event is sent.

We have enabled Google Signals, which lets Google associate activity across devices for visitors who are signed in to a Google account and have allowed ad personalization. You can control this at Google My Ad Center.

2. Advertising

We may show ads served by Google (Google AdSense) and may advertise the site using Google Ads. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising at Google My Ad Center or aboutads.info. See how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

3. Consent and regional choices

For visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, analytics and advertising cookies are off by default and only turn on if you agree through a consent message. For visitors in Quebec, advertising cookies are off by default. For other visitors, analytics and advertising operate as described here, and you can opt out at any time (see section 7).

4. Plant orders and pre-orders

If you place an order or pre-order, we collect the name, contact email or phone number, and pickup notes you provide, along with the plants ordered. This is stored in our Google Sheets order log and emailed to us so we can confirm your order and arrange pickup. It is never sent to Google Analytics or used for advertising. Orders and pickup are available to British Columbia residents only, and all live plant sales are final.

5. Data retention

Detailed Google Analytics event data is kept for up to 14 months. Aggregated, non-identifying analytics and search-performance data may be kept longer in Google Cloud BigQuery for long-term trend analysis. Order records are kept as long as needed for order fulfillment and basic bookkeeping, then deleted on request where the law allows.

6. Service providers

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and uses Google services (Analytics, Tag Manager, Search Console, Sheets, Apps Script, BigQuery, AdSense/Ads), Google Fonts, and embedded content such as Google Maps and Spotify, which may set their own cookies when you interact with them.

7. Your choices

  • Turn analytics off on this browser: open Privacy & Analytics settings.
  • Install the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.
  • Block or delete cookies in your browser settings.
  • Ask to access, correct, or delete personal information we hold about you.
