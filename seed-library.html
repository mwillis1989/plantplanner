Surrey Royal Heights Seed & Book Library

A free, self-serve neighbourhood seed & book exchange at 11531 97 Ave, Surrey, BC — part of the West Coast Plant Project.

How It Works

  • Take seeds or books you’ll actually use this season.
  • Share back saved seeds (labelled with plant name & year), surplus seed packets, and gardening books in good condition.
  • Native & pollinator seeds are especially welcome — see the planting calendar for when to sow.

Community Rules & Etiquette

  • Take only what you need to share resources fairly.
  • Respect the space and keep it tidy.
  • Always close the doors securely after your visit.
  • Donations are welcome if they are in good condition.

Find the Library

11531 97 Ave, Surrey, BC V3V 2B5 (Royal Heights neighbourhood). Also an official PokéStop!
