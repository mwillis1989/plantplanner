Overview

Summary

Rhizomatous Perennial Forb, 20-40 cm height with vanilla-scented dried leaves with 3 fan-shaped leaflets and white flowers. Inhabiting forests, specifically moist to mesic open or closed forests in the lowland and montane zones, this species is frequent in southwestern British Columbia and is documented from Vancouver Island, the Gulf Islands, and the adjacent lower mainland, extending south to northwestern California.

Botanical Description

Distinctive deciduous perennial herb that forms a slender rhizome in the understorey of the forest. Vanilla Leaf is best known because its dried leaves have a vanilla-like fragrance. Each leaf consists of 3 horizontally-oriented leaflets, similar to Trilliums. An attractive forest understory perennial herb spreading from delicate creeping rhizomes, reaching 20–40 cm in height.

Name Etymology

Genus Achlys; species Achlys triphylla.
