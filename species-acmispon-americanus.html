Spanish-clover

Overview

Summary

Annual Forb, 15-40 cm height with white flowers.

Botanical Description

A delicate, low-lying annual forb, it is locally confined to clay soils in the Victoria area. In good conditions, plants will sprawl and form a summer groundcover. It is a possible host plant for the Grey Hairstreak butterfly. A low-growing, sprawling annual legume rising 15–40 cm, featuring pinnately compound leaves with 3–4 small ovate leaflets and solitary, creamy-white pea-like flowers that attract native pollinators. Nitrogen-fixing.

Mycorrhizal Association

Competition / Allelopathy

Symbiotic nitrogen-fixer that elevates site fertility and accelerates canopy closure; in low-nutrient Garry oak or alpine meadows, space carefully so vigorous nitrogen responders do not crowd slow forbs.
