Western Maiden-hair Fern

Adiantum aleuticum L. · Pteridaceae

🌿 NativeBC CDC: YellowFernZone 4a–9b🦋 19 Lepidoptera hosts
🔎 Open 5-Tab Explorer Profile📊 Compare in Native Plant MatrixE-Flora BC ↗

Overview

Summary

Moist forests, rocks, scree, cliffs, banks and waterfall spray zones in the lowland and montane zones; frequent throughout BC, mostly S of 55°N, infrequent in SC BC; amphiberingian, N to AK, E to SW AB and S to CO, AZ and CA; disjunct E to PQ and NF and S to PA, WV and MI. Amphiberingian, ranging north to Alaska, east to southwestern Alberta, and south to Colorado, Arizona, and California; disjunct east to Quebec and Newfoundland and south to Pennsylvania, West Virginia, and Michigan; also in East Asia.

Botanical Description

An elegant native deciduous fern growing 15–75 cm tall from creeping scaly rhizomes. Features wiry, polished purplish-black stipes that fork into horizontal, fan-like radiating fronds with delicate lobed pinnules. Deciduous perennial, palmately branched, from a stout, scaly rhizome, 15-75 cm tall.
