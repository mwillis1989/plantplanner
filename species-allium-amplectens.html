Slimleaf Onion

Allium amplectens Torr. · Amaryllidaceae

🌿 NativeBC CDC: YellowWildflower (Forb)Zone 4a–8b🦋 92 Lepidoptera hosts
🔎 Open 5-Tab Explorer Profile📊 Compare in Native Plant MatrixE-Flora BC ↗

Overview

Summary

Perennial Forb from bulb, 10 – 40 cm height with white to pink saucer-shaped flowers. Found in rocky bluffs and meadows, specifically inhabiting vernally moist rocky bluffs and meadows in the lowland zone, this species is infrequent on southeastern Vancouver Island, the Gulf Islands, and the adjacent mainland at Powell River, extending south to California.

Botanical Description

This compact perennial emerges from a unique bulb with a network of wavy scales. Its delicate, slender stems rise to a height of 10-40 cm, showcasing clusters of distinctive white to pink, saucer-shaped flowers. A slender perennial herb rising 10–40 cm tall from an egg-shaped bulb wrapped in a distinctive wavy, fibrous-meshed coat.

Name Etymology

Allium is Latin for garlic, and amplectens means embracing, referring to its clasping leaves.
