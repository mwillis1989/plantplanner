Hare Sedge

Carex ovalis · Cyperaceae

Overview

Summary

Tufted perennial sedge belonging to the species-rich Ovalis Tribe, growing 20-50 cm tall with fine green foliage and green/brown dry single-seeded fruits. Meadows, seasonally wet soils.

Botanical Description

This is one of our local sedges in the species-rich Ovalis Tribe that grows commonly in fairly moist clay-rich full sun meadows. Tufted perennial sedge belonging to the species-rich Ovalis Tribe, growing 20-50 cm tall with fine green foliage and green/brown achenes. Tufted perennial sedge (20–50 cm tall) with fine green foliage and clustered brownish spikelets.
