Small-flowered Blue-eyed Mary

Collinsia parviflora · Plantaginaceae

🌿 NativeBC CDC: YellowWildflower (Forb)Zone 4a–8b🦋 2 Lepidoptera hosts

Overview

Summary

Annual Forb from tap root, 5-50 cm height; opposite leaves purplish underneath; terminal cluster of short-stalked 2-lipped flowers (upper white, lower blue). Inhabiting rock outcrops, meadows, and sandy beaches, as well as vernally moist to dry grassy slopes, mossy rock outcrops, forest glades, and open forests in the lowland and montane zones (low to mid elevations, tolerating temperatures down to -36°C).

Botanical Description

Annual herb arising from a taproot; stems ascending to erect, sometimes sprawling, 5-50 cm tall/long, slender, simple or branched, and minutely hairy. Leaves opposite, smooth or minutely hairy, and frequently purplish beneath; lower leaves egg- to spoon-shaped, borne on 5- to 10 mm-long stalks; middle and upper leaves oblong to linear-lanceolate, 1-4 cm long, entire or nearly so, becoming smaller, linear, bract-like, and often whorled in the inflorescence. Small annual herb (5–50 cm tall) with opposite leaves (purplish underneath) and 2-lipped blue and white flowers. Annual Forb from tap root, 5-50 cm height; terminal cluster of short-stalked 2-lipped flowers (upper white, lower blue).

Habitat & BC Range

Natural Habitat

Inhabiting rock outcrops, meadows, and sandy beaches, as well as vernally moist to dry grassy slopes, mossy rock outcrops, forest glades, and open forests in the lowland and montane zones (low to mid elevations, tolerating temperatures down to -36°C). Common throughout British Columbia except the northeast; ranging north to Alaska and southern Yukon, east to Ontario, and south to Pennsylvania, Michigan, South Dakota, New Mexico, Arizona, and California.

Forests; Meadows; Rock Outcrops & Bluffs; Wetlands & Marshes; Beaches & Dunes

Garden Design & Growing

Garden Uses

Meadow; Woodland / Shade; Rock Garden; Rain Garden / Wetland; Coastal / Bluff

Key Features

Pollinator Magnet; Butterfly / Moth Host; Erosion Control; Cover Crop / Green Manure

Soil Preferences

Moist to dry grassy slopes, mossy rock outcrops, and open forest glades; shallow dry nitrogen-medium soils. Dry to mesic.

Moisture Notes

Prefers evenly moist, well-drained soils without standing winter water.

Spread Behaviour

Deep-rooted non-spreading clump

Root Formation

Taproot

Mycorrhizal Association

Arbuscular Mycorrhizal (AM; Glomeromycota endomycorrhizal network)

Competition / Allelopathy

Non-allelopathic and non-aggressive (Taproot); low root competition makes it safe for intimate mixed borders and diverse polyculture interplanting.

Erosion & Slope Control

Cover crop for green-up and erosion control with minimal competition; seed mixtures for diversity.
