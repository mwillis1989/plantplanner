Overview

Summary

This perennial bunchgrass reaches 15-90 cm in height with green flowers, featuring a tufted habit from fibrous roots. Inhabiting rocky outcrops and meadows on mesic to dry, open slopes in the lowland and montane zones, this species is infrequent in southwestern British Columbia and rare in south-central British Columbia, extending south to Montana, Arizona, and California.

Botanical Description

This perennial bunchgrass reaches 15-90 cm in height with green flowers, featuring a tufted habit from fibrous roots.

Name Etymology

Genus Eriocoma; specific epithet lemmonii honors a botanical collector or naturalist associated with Lemmon's Needle Grass.
