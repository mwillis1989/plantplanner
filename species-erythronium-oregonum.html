Overview

Summary

Perennial Forb from bulb, 15-35 cm height with mottled leaves and white flowers with yellow centres tipping downward (petals curl upward with age). Found in coastal bluffs, meadows, thickets, and open woodlands, as well as dry to mesic meadows and open forests in the lowland zone; locally common on southeastern Vancouver Island, the Gulf Islands, and the adjacent mainland, extending south to southern Oregon.

Botanical Description

Perennial Forb from bulb, 15-35 cm height with mottled leaves and white flowers with yellow centres tipping downward (petals curl upward with age). Perennial herb from a narrowly egg-shaped, 3- to 5-cm long bulb and an elongate, segmented corm; flowering stems measure 15-35 cm tall and are smooth. Basal leaves number 2, are dull dark green mottled with brown and white or pale green, lanceolate to oblong-elliptic, 10-20 cm long, smooth, abruptly narrowed to short, broad, somewhat winged stalks, with margins that are entire but often somewhat wavy; stem leaves are lacking. Perennial bulbous geophyte (15–35 cm tall) with mottled leaves and white nodding flowers with yellow centers.

Root Formation
