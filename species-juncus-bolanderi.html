Bolander's Rush

Overview

Summary

Rhizomatous Perennial Rush, 30-50 cm height with round leaves, bracts, and brown flowers. Found in meadows and wetlands, specifically wet meadows and lakeshores in the lowland zone; infrequent in southwestern British Columbia and rare in southeastern British Columbia, ranging south to California.

Botanical Description

At first glance, you might mistakenly think you're looking at the equally distinctive Dagger-leaf Rush. At closer inspection, the round leaves and bracts are a giveaway that you are looking at Bolander's Rush. This rush prefers wet soils in full sun. A rhizomatous perennial rush forming loose tufts from short rhizomes, 30-50 cm in height, with erect stems 40-75 cm tall.
