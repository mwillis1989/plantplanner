Overview

Summary

Coarse coastal perennial grass, 50-150 cm tall, forming large clumps from thick, ascending rhizomes, featuring blueish-green blades and yellow flowers; stems erect, stout, 50-150 cm tall, usually finely but copiously hairy or sometimes smooth above. Moist to mesic sandy or gravelly beaches and shoreline forests in the lowland zone; common in coastal BC; amphiberingian, N to AK, YT and N, E to NF and S to NH, PA, MI, IL and CA.

Botanical Description

Coarse coastal perennial grass, 50-150 cm tall, forming large clumps from thick, ascending rhizomes, featuring blueish-green blades and yellow flowers; stems erect, stout, 50-150 cm tall, usually finely but copiously hairy or sometimes smooth above. Sheaths open, smooth; blades tough, flat to in-rolled, 6-15 mm wide, with numerous prominent nerves, very finely minutely rough appressed-hairy on the upper surfaces and smooth underneath; ear-shaped lobes usually developed at some leaf-bases; ligules more or less fringed with fine hairs, scarcely 1 mm long. Coarse, blue-green perennial grass (50–150 cm tall) with thick creeping rhizomes and heavy terminal spikes. Coarse coastal dune grass.

Moist to mesic sandy or gravelly beaches and shoreline forests in the lowland zone; common in coastal BC; amphiberingian, N to AK, YT and N, E to NF and S to NH, PA, MI, IL and CA.
