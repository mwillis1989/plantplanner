Alaska Oniongrass

Melica subulata · Poaceae

Overview

Summary

Perennial, tufted grass characterized by deep green leaves, purple-green striated flowers, and short, thick, bulbous-based rhizomes with clustered bulbs. Forests and woodlands.

Botanical Description

Perennial grass (30-80 cm) with deep green leaves and purple-green striated flowers. Stems are erect, reaching 30-80 (110) cm in height. Sheaths are closed nearly or quite to the tops (though often split at maturity) and range from sparsely to copiously spreading-hairy to minutely rough-hairy. Leaf blades are flat, 2-7 mm wide, minutely rough- to crisp-hairy or sparsely stiff-hairy on the upper surfaces, and minutely rough to smooth beneath.

Coast Salish Names

Saxwul / SOX̱EL (sáx̣ʷəl) is a general Coast Salish term covering all grasses rather than a species-specific noun. General grass term (saxwul / SOX̱EL sáx̣ʷəl); no species-specific noun attested.

Habitat & BC Range

Natural Habitat

Forests and woodlands. Inhabiting moist to dry meadows and forests in the lowland and montane zones, this species is common on Vancouver Island, frequent on the Queen Charlotte Islands, and rare in southern British Columbia, extending north to southeastern Alaska, east to southwestern Alberta, and south to South Dakota, Idaho, Utah, and California, with an additional occurrence in Chile.
