Big-leaved Sandwort

Moehringia macrophylla · Caryophyllaceae

🌿 NativeBC CDC: YellowWildflower (Forb)Zone 4a–8b🦋 2 Lepidoptera hosts

Overview

Summary

Occurring in moist to dry forests and rocky slopes across lowland, steppe, and montane zones; common in extreme southern British Columbia, rare northward to 55°N; extending north to Northwest Territories, east to Quebec, and south to New Mexico and California.

Botanical Description

Perennial herb from an extensive slender rhizome; often forming loosely matted patches with many sterile as well as floriferous stems; stems decumbent to erect, several, usually branching, short rough-hairy, 3- or often 4-angled, 5-25 cm long. Stems are decumbent to erect, several, usually branching, short rough-hairy, 3- or often 4-angled, and 5-25 cm long. Basal leaves are lacking; stem leaves are opposite, numerous, linear-elliptic to lanceolate, abruptly sharp-pointed, 2-5 cm long, 2-12 mm wide, and essentially glabrous; stipules are lacking.
