Overview

Summary

Perennial Forb, 10-30 cm height with pink-coloured reflexed petals. Meadows and rocky slopes.

Botanical Description

Perennial Forb, 10-30 cm height with pink-coloured reflexed petals. Requiring more moisture than its close relative, Broad-leaved Shootingstar, this perennial forb grows 10-30 cm in height and typically inhabits moist to seasonally wet meadows and seeps in full sun. Its typical habitat is moist to seasonally wet meadows and seeps in full sun.
