Other Ways to Contribute

Donating supplies, plants, or knowledge helps just as much as pitching in financially.

Live Divisions, Cuttings & Pots

Have vigorous native perennials, berry canes, clean 4-inch / 1-gallon nursery pots, or propagation trays to spare? Reach out by email to arrange a drop-off in Surrey.

Botanical & Gardening Books

Gently used West Coast flora guides, pruning handbooks, soil biology texts, and children's nature books are always welcome for the community shelf.

Field Research & Local Data

Share verified soil pH readings, Coast Salish spelling corrections, germination trials, or insect observations to strengthen the open botanical dataset.
