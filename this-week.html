✅ Garden To-Do List

  • First frost arrives early–mid November: protect tender plants and empty hoses.
  • Mulch garlic beds with leaves or straw.
  • Direct-sow cold-stratifying native seeds (camas, lupine, nodding onion) outdoors.
  • Clean, sharpen and oil tools before storing them.
